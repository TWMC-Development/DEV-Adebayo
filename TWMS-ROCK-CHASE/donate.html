SUPPORT OUR SCHOOL: MAKE A DIFFERENCE TODAY!

Welcome to Our Donation Page!


Why Your Support Matters

At The Woodlands Methodist School, our mission is to provide a joyful, Christian education through which children develop a lifelong love of learning, pursue excellence, and positively impact the world. Your support plays a crucial role in helping us achieve this mission and provide our students with exceptional programming and resources.

How You Can Help

Employer Gift Matching

Many companies offer donation-matching programs which can sometimes double or even triple your gift. Please contact your employer for information about matching programs.


One-Time Donation

Make a single contribution to support our current needs and initiatives. Every gift, large or small, has a significant impact.


Recurring Giving

Set up a recurring donation to provide ongoing support. Weekly, bi-weekly or monthly contributions help us plan and budget for long-term projects and programs.


HOW TO DONATE

Online

Use our secure online donation form. You can choose the amount and frequency of your donation and provide your payment details safely. Please scroll down to “School - The Woodlands Methodist School” to enter your preferred donation amount.

DONATE NOW

Text to Give:

Text 281.500.8400 and enter ‘twms’ and a dollar amount in the message field. Example: twms 100


By Mail
