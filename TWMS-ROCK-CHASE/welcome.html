WELCOME

WELCOME FROM THE HEAD OF SCHOOL

With a heart for servant leadership and a deep commitment to each child’s growth and potential, I warmly invite you to explore our website and become acquainted with The Woodlands Methodist School. Here, we joyfully guide and nurture children from prekindergarten through eighth grade, placing students at the center of all we do as we partner with their families to support their growth in faith, character and learning.

Rooted in our mission to reach students for Christ and disciple them as they walk with Him, our school is a place where faith and learning come together to shape confident, compassionate and Christ-centered leaders. Children at The Woodlands Methodist School develop a lifelong love of learning, pursue excellence, and positively impact the world.

As you learn more about our school community, you will discover an environment where students are challenged to think independently, explore new ideas, and take risks with courage and enthusiasm. We strive to inspire each child to serve others with passion and commitment and to lead with integrity, grounded in a steadfast faith. As Isaiah 40:31 reminds us, “Those who hope in the Lord will renew their strength. They will soar on wings like eagles; they will run and not grow weary, they will walk and not be faint.” This promise reflects our vision for every student to grow in faith, persevere with strength, and pursue excellence in all they do.

Our caring and remarkable faculty and staff bring this vision to life every day, modeling Christ-centered leadership and an unwavering commitment to every student’s growth. In partnership with our church staff, we gather weekly for Chapel and continue to serve as an outreach ministry of The Woodlands Methodist Church, nurturing our students’ spiritual development alongside their academic journey.

As Head of School, I am truly honored to serve this remarkable community. It is a privilege to work alongside passionate educators who faithfully live out our mission each day. Throughout our campus, you will see their expertise and dedication reflected in our programs and enrichment opportunities across prekindergarten through eighth grade.

We believe that a child’s education is a shared journey, and we warmly welcome your partnership and involvement as your child grows spiritually, academically and personally.

I invite you to visit The Woodlands Methodist School and experience firsthand what makes our community so special. We are grateful for the opportunity to welcome you and your family to our campus. Please do not hesitate to reach out with any questions, thoughts or feedback at any time. May the Lord’s grace and peace be with you and your family throughout the year.

With gratitude and blessings,

CHRIS POVICH
Head of School
