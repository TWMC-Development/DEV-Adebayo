APPLY

ABOUT

Applications for elementary and middle school are currently being accepted for the 2025-2026 school year. Please call for additional information. Assessments are required for all students. Students are placed in classes based on their age as of September 1st of the current school year, consistent with Texas public school placement.

HOW TO APPLY

Applying is easy using our online application. A non-refundable fee must be submitted with each application as follows:

CREATE AN ONLINE APPLICATION

To begin the online application process, create an account. Then log in to your account and create a new student application for your child. You will then have the flexibility to log in and out of your account and access your open application. (Please note that if you already have another child enrolled in our school, you will not need to create another account. Just click Online Application/Login and enter your existing login and password.)

REQUEST MORE INFORMATION

Should you desire more information than is available on our website, please request more information and our office will contact you.

WE LOOK FORWARD TO MEETING YOU

If you have not already done so, please contact us to schedule a tour. We would very much like to meet you and show you around our beautiful campus. We appreciate your interest and hope to assist you in any way we can. If you have questions, please feel free to contact us at admission@twmc.org.

ENROLLMENT

Newly admitted students complete the enrollment process via their online Family Portal account. The non-refundable enrollment fee is paid at the time the online enrollment forms are completed and submitted.

CONTINUOUS ENROLLMENT

Continuous Enrollment simplifies the entire enrollment process. Families complete enrollment one time when they initially enroll. Essentially, once that initial enrollment is complete, your student will have a spot at TWMS until graduation after 8th grade or until the family completes a withdrawal form.

For families, this means fewer deadlines, less paperwork, and no worry over racing to enroll for coveted space in a full classroom. For TWMS, Continuous Enrollment allows us to better budget and forecast needs for the future.

Even more important to TWMS, is knowing that we have a commitment to partner together in the education of your student. A long-range plan is essential to the process of education, provides stability in our classrooms and helps us plan for the future.

TWMS CONTINUOUS ENROLLMENT FAQs

What is continuous enrollment?

Continuous enrollment is a simplified process that is paperless and convenient. Continuous enrollment means that when you enroll your student, you are enrolling from that date until your child graduates from 8th grade.

How does this differ from reenrollment?

Continuous enrollment means less work for our families. Families will no longer have to complete a reenrollment packet every year, saving them time and effort. Your enrollment and payment information will automatically carry forward to the next school year.

What if we don’t plan to return the next school year, or we need more time to decide?

Simply submit the “Intent to Withdraw Form” by the deadline to avoid enrolling for the following school year and paying the $350 non-refundable enrollment fee. You may re-enroll later if space is available, and you will be charged a $450 non-refundable enrollment fee at that time.

What about policy and tuition changes?

Policies are detailed in our handbook which can be found on the school website. Tuition will be published on the website in December prior to the withdrawal deadline. It is the parents’ responsibility to review the handbook for any policy changes.

If my child is continuously enrolled, can the school choose to unenroll my child?

The Woodlands Methodist School reserves the right to withdraw a student at any time due to student performance or behavior issues, or if families have delinquent financial obligations.

What if I need to update my child’s information (address, phone, emergency pickup, medical information)?

Any demographic or emergency contact update can be made in your Family Portal account. Requests to update medical history and acknowledge acceptance of school policies will be sent to families prior to the start of school each year.

What if I wish to change my tuition payment plan or need to update my financial account?

Requests to change your payment plan or update your financial information can be made by contacting the Financial Office. The annual tuition payment plan must be updated and approved by August 1st.
