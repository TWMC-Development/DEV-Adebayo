At The Woodlands Methodist School, we view the arts as a vital expression of God’s grace and creativity, and we are committed to helping our students discover and cultivate their own artistic gifts. Our fine arts opportunities inspire students through a diverse and enriching array of artistic disciplines, including visual arts, music, choir, band, and drama.

Our dedicated faculty members bring a wealth of experience and passion to their teaching, guiding students to explore their creative potential while embedding a strong Christian foundation into their artistic journey. Whether you are a budding painter, a gifted musician, or a talented actor, our school provides a supportive and stimulating environment where you can develop your skills, express your unique vision, and grow in your faith.
