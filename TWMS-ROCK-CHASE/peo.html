P.E.O.

ELEMENTARY AND MIDDLE SCHOOL

Parents are an integral part of our school and its success! We value the input and collaboration that occurs when parents are involved with their child’s education. Parents can be actively involved with our Parent Enrichment Organization that plans and implements programs and activities for our school. The P.E.O. meets monthly throughout the school year. 

The Woodlands Methodist School P.E.O. Fundraiser

Your TWMS PEO is spearheading FIESTA FOR THE FUTURE with a GOAL OF RAISING $500,000 for new outdoor turf and refreshed school gym flooring to support students now and for generations of Eagles to come. With the support of our school community, we’re creating spaces that encourage teamwork, confidence, and joyful activity every day.
