P.E.O.

ELEMENTARY AND MIDDLE SCHOOL

Parents are an integral part of our school and its success! We value the input and collaboration that occurs when parents are involved with their child’s education. Parents can be actively involved with our Parent Enrichment Organization that plans and implements programs and activities for our school. The P.E.O. meets monthly throughout the school year. 

The Woodlands Methodist School P.E.O. Invites You To

Date May 5, 2026 Time 7:00-10:00 PM Location Mercedes-Benz of The Woodlands

Join us for an evening of celebration, community and purpose at the TWMS PEO’s Fiesta for the Future, a signature fundraiser held on Cinco de Mayo. Guests will enjoy a festive atmosphere while helping raise funds for new outdoor turf and updated gym flooring where current and future TWMS Eagles will be encouraged to “run with perseverance the race set before them.” (Hebrews 12:1)

Complimentary valet parking will be available.
