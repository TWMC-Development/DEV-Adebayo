TUITION ASSISTANCE

The Woodlands Methodist School has a needs-based tuition assistance program to assist enrolled families during a difficult financial season. CLICK HERE to read our Tuition Assistance Policy and find information needed to apply. Tuition Assistance applications are submitted online via FACTS Family Portal after a student is enrolled for the 2024-25 school year. The tuition assistance application link is available in January each year and closes on March 20, 2024. Applications received after the first-round deadline are processed on a first come, first served basis as funds permit.



WAITING LIST

A waiting list is maintained for those interested in enrolling when space becomes available. Each waiting list is for one school year only, ending in May.
