Missions

Until the Whole World Knows

Our Calling

Reach People for Jesus Christ, Disciple Them in Faith, Help Those in Need

Missions is the heartbeat of our church. It is interwoven in and throughout all of our ministries. It's how we live out the Great Commission found in Matthew 28:19–20. And because we love Jesus Christ, we have hearts of compassion to help those in need.

Our ministries empower people in need, giving them dignity, self-worth and the ability to become strong and self-sufficient. We work both globally and locally to faithfully serve God's people.
