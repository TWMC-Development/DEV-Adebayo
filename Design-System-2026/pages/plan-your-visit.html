We Can't Wait to Meet You

Your First Visit

You Belong Here

Whether you've been going to church your whole life or you're just starting to explore faith, you're welcome here. At The Woodlands Methodist Church, we believe that everyone has a place and a purpose — and we'd love to help you find yours.

Your first visit is all about you. There's no pressure, no commitment — just a warm, friendly environment where you can experience authentic community and hear a message that matters. We can't wait to meet you.

Know Before You Go

What to Expect

Parking

Free parking at all campuses. Look for First Time Guest signs — our parking team will direct you to reserved spots near the entrance.

Kids Programs

Safe, age-appropriate programs for infants through 5th grade during every service. Check in when you arrive and we'll get your family set up.

Come as You Are

No dress code. You'll see everything from jeans and t-shirts to business casual. Wear whatever makes you comfortable.

Service Length

Services typically last 60–75 minutes with worship music, a message, and communion on the first Sunday of each month.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

There is no dress code at TWMC. You'll see everything from jeans and t-shirts to business casual. Wear whatever makes you comfortable — we're just glad you're here.

Safe, age-appropriate programming is available for infants through 5th grade during every service at every campus. When you arrive, look for the NextGen Kids check-in area and our team will get your family set up. All of our volunteers are background-checked.

Most services last 60–75 minutes. You can expect a mix of worship music, a message from one of our pastors, and opportunities to connect. Communion is offered on the first Sunday of each month.

Free parking is available at all campuses. Look for the "First Time Guest" signs and our parking team will direct you to reserved spots near the entrance. If you need accessible parking, just let a team member know.

Just yourself! Bibles are available, song lyrics are projected on screen, and giving is handled online. Everything you need is provided. We'll even have coffee ready for you.

Greeters are stationed at every entrance and throughout the building. Look for anyone with a lanyard or name badge — they'll be happy to help you find your way, answer questions, or walk you to your seat.
