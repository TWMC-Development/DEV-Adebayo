Generosity

Why We Give

At The Woodlands Methodist Church, generosity is part of who we are, rooted in the love and grace of God. Your generosity helps us live out our mission and extend God's love beyond our walls and into the world.

Reaching People for Jesus

Share the gospel with those who need it most.

Discipling Them in Faith

Provide resources and opportunities for spiritual growth.

Helping Those in Need

Support ministries, outreach programs and missions.

Give Now Recurring Gift

We are generous because the gospel is generous to us.

God's greatest gift to us was His Son, Jesus.

For God so loved the world that he gave his one and only Son, that whoever believes in him shall not perish but have eternal life. — John 3:16

Giving is about what God wants for you, not from you.

Our giving flows from a heart of gratitude, not obligation — giving out of joy for what God has done for us.

Each of you should use whatever gift you have received to serve others, as faithful stewards of God's grace in its various forms. — 1 Peter 4:10

Generosity is transformational, not transactional.

When we give, we participate in something far greater than ourselves.

Store up for yourselves treasures in heaven. For where your treasure is, there your heart will be also. — Matthew 6:20–21

Your Options

Ways to Give

Give Online

Checks

Write a check payable to The Woodlands Methodist Church. Place in a worship offering or mail to: 2200 Lake Woodlands Drive, The Woodlands, TX 77380. Note your campus in the memo.

Text to Give

Text a dollar amount to 281.500.8400. Use keywords like HARVEST, LOFT, ESPANOL, WOODFOREST, MONTGOMERY, CREEKSIDE, MISSIONS, or SOSS followed by an amount.

Honorariums & Memorials

Make a one-time donation to the TWMC Foundation in honor or memory of a loved one.

Securities

Gifts of long-term securities generally result in federal tax benefits. Contact the Finance Office for brokerage account information.

Donate a Vehicle

Contact Jonah Robson on the Missions Team for more information about donating a vehicle to support the church's mission.

Leave a Legacy

Give to The Woodlands Methodist Church Foundation. Contact the Finance Office to discuss estate gifts and bequests.
