Easter Arrival Tips – Helping Everyone Experience a Warm Welcome

As we prepare for Easter, we are joyfully anticipating welcoming many new and returning worshippers to our campus. In an effort to create a smooth and comfortable arrival and departure experience for everyone, we want to share a few helpful reminders:

Parking Details

View Campus Map

Campus Map

If you are able, please consider parking on the third and fourth levels of the Whole Foods parking garage. For your convenience, two shuttles will run continuously between the garage and our campus with three designated stops:

  • The east end of the parking garage (corner of Lake Front Circle & Aviator Drive)
  • The Harvest Worship Center
  • The Loft Worship Center

If you prefer to walk, the Harvest Worship Center is just a short and easy walk across Lake Front Circle from the east stairwell of the garage.


Avoid
To help ease congestion, please avoid Entrance 4 off Grogan’s Mill Road near the Loft Building.

Traffic Flow
Please remember that you may only make right-hand turns when exiting The Woodlands campus. Thank you for being mindful of traffic flow and our surrounding community.

Golf Carts
If you park farther from the buildings than is comfortable, our Golf Cart Ministry is happy to serve you. They are there to help make your arrival and departure easier.

Be Neighborly
Easter brings many first-time guests. Let’s extend patience, kindness, and generosity in the parking lot so that from the very moment they arrive, they experience the difference Jesus has made in our lives—and can make in theirs.

Thank You
Thank you for the many ways you sacrificially support The Woodlands Methodist Church. Because of you, we continue to reach people for Christ, disciple them in faith, and help people in need. We look forward to celebrating the risen Savior together!
