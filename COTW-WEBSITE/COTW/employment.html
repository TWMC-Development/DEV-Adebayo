Developmental Specialist

ESSENTIAL FUNCTIONS:
The Developmental Specialist helps to provide the best possible learning environment for children. This includes:

Relational Leadership

  • Support mission statement, guiding principles and school philosophy

Strategic/Operational Leadership

  • Support, encourage and provide training for early childhood classroom staff in inclusion practices
  • Support and guide parents regarding behavior concerns and developmental milestones
  • Support, guide and provide resources for parents of children with special needs
  • Support individual student needs and developmentally appropriate practices within the program
  • Collaborate with families, school staff, and outside professionals to meet student needs
  • Meet COTW and licensing requirements for in-service training and professional growth

QUALIFICATIONS:

  • Bachelor’s degree or higher in early childhood or special education or related field
  • Five years of experience in early childhood special education
  • Committed Christian
  • Strong interpersonal skills

PHYSICAL DEMANDS:

  • This job requires bending, sitting, reaching, standing, lifting, and crawling.

OTHER SPECIFICATIONS:

  • Reports to: Director of Children of The Woodlands
  • Position Type: Part time, not to exceed 15 hours per week
  • Schedule: Available to work a flexible schedule during school hours (Monday –Thursday, 9am -2pm); not to exceed 15 hours per week
  • Compensation: Dependent on experience
  • Benefits: Not eligible for benefits

This job description is intended to describe the general nature of this job and may not include all responsibilities that might be required of the person holding this position. This job description is subject to change at any time at the sole discretion of Children of The Woodlands / The Woodlands Methodist Church and does not establish a contract for employment.

APPLICATION:

Submit resume and cover letter to (employment@twmc.org).

Resource Coordinator

SUMMARY:
Provide support to the Director of COTW for resource planning, purchasing, and organizing. Support mission statement, guiding principles and school philosophy.

ESSENTIAL FUNCTIONS:
Collaborate in planning, purchasing, and managing inventory of non-consumable and consumable supplies and equipment. This includes:

Relational Leadership

  • Support preschool mission statement, guiding principles and school philosophy
  • Assist with special events

Strategic/Operational Leadership

  • Coordinate use of preschool storage, including maintenance and organization of supply closets
  • Coordinate with preschool director and age-level supervisors regarding supply needs and requests
  • Shop for and order approved supplies as requested, operating within set budget
  • Reconcile monthly Nexonia report, request payments as needed, and maintain invoice files
  • Serve as the point person for preschool equipment such as the laminator, copier and die cut
  • Assist and cooperate with other church programs regarding sharing space and supplies
  • Liaison with TWMC facilities and janitorial contractor regarding preschool needs and submit work orders
  • Oversee playgrounds and equipment, including daily safety checks and monthly playground report
  • Be available for classrooms that need assistance or if a staff person needs to leave the classroom, including courtesy breaks
  • Cover last minute substitute needs for staff
  • Assist with preschool arrival and departure
  • Meet licensing and school requirements for training and professional growth in a timely manner
  • Perform other duties as requested

QUALIFICATIONS:

  • Bachelor’s degree preferred
  • Strong interpersonal skills
  • Strong organizational skills
  • Strong computer skills including a working knowledge of Microsoft Office
  • Excellent written communication skills
  • Preschool experience preferred
  • Ability to work well in a team, as well as work independently
  • Must be a committed Christian comfortable sharing their faith with children and families
  • Must meet licensing requirements, which includes fingerprinting and an extensive background check

PHYSICAL DEMANDS:

The physical demands described here are representative of those that must be met by and employee to successfully perform the essential functions for this job. While performing the duties of this job, the employee is regularly required to talk and hear. This position is very active and requires standing, walking, bending, kneeling, stooping, crouching, crawling, and climbing all day. The employee must frequently lift and / or move items over 30 pounds. Specific vision abilities required by this job include close vision, distance vision, color vision, peripheral vision, depth perception and ability to adjust focus.

OTHER SPECIFICATIONS:

  • Reports to: Director of Children of The Woodlands
  • Position Type: Part time, year round
  • Schedule: As is typical for ministry, this position requires a flexible schedule. It includes agreed upon onsite office hours of 5 hours per day Wednesday / Thursday / Friday during the 2022 – 2023 school year. Some summer hours are required, and those hours will vary. During the school year 2023 – 2024, it includes agreed upon onsite office hours of 5 hours per day Monday / Wednesday / Thursday. Some summer hours are required, and those hours will vary.
  • Compensation: Dependent on experience
  • Benefits: Not benefit eligible

This job description is intended to describe the general nature of this job and may not include all responsibilities that might be required of the person holding this position. This job description is subject to change at any time at the sole discretion of Children of The Woodlands / The Woodlands Methodist Church and does not establish a contract for employment.

APPLICATION:

To be considered for this position, please submit a resume and letter of interest to Children of The Woodlands (preschoolbusiness@twmc.org).

COTW Nurse Substitute

Department: Children of The Woodlands ‐ Preschool
Reports to: Lead Nurse

SCOPE:

Children of The Woodlands Preschool is a ministry of The Woodlands Methodist Church. They are currently seeking candidates for Nurse Substitute positions for the 2022-2023 school year. This position is responsible for providing the best possible care for our children when one of our nurses is away. Nurse Substitutes must be able to meet all state licensing requirements as well as accreditation criteria. They must also be committed Christians. This is a part-time position that is not benefits eligible.

DUTIES & RESPONSIBILITIES:

  • Developing plans for student care based on assessment, interventions, and identification of outcomes, and the evaluation of care
  • Cares for students with chronic illnesses developing a health care plan with the physician and parents. Communicates and coordinates the needs of the student with the teachers
  • Provide care for students with acute illnesses and injuries
  • Monitoring immunizations, managing communicable diseases, and assessing the school environment to prevent injury and ensure safety
  • Overseeing infection control measures
  • Actively participating in school safety plans that address school violence, bullying, and emergencies that may occur at school
  • Overseeing medication administration, health care procedures, and the development of healthcare plans
  • Making decisions related to the delegation of healthcare tasks as directed by state laws and professional practice guidance
  • Providing health advice to school educational teams, such as the Counselor and Developmental Specialist teams in developing plans and IP’s for students.
  • Assists in maintaining first aid kits for each classroom
  • First responder for life threatening emergencies.

QUALIFICATIONS:

  • Bachelor’s degree and current Texas Registered Nursing License
  • Minimum of two years of experience in Pediatric Health Care
  • Current Pediatric CPR and First Aid certification
  • Growing Christian
  • Must have a heart for working with children
  • Must have strong oral and written communication skills
  • Working knowledge of Microsoft Office and ability to learn new programs quickly

PHYSICAL DEMANDS:

  • The physical demands described here are representative of those that must be met by an employee to successfully perform the essential functions of this job.
  • While performing the duties of this job, the employee is regularly required to talk and hear. This position is very active and requires standing, walking, bending, kneeling, stooping, crouching, crawling, and climbing all day. The employee must be able to lift and/or move items over 30 pounds. Specific vision abilities required by this job include close vision, distance vision, color vision, peripheral vision, depth perception and ability to adjust focus.

CLASSIFICATION:

  • Part-Time/Non-Exempt; not to exceed 29 hours/week
  • This position is not eligible for benefits
  • Temporary position; as needed basis

APPLICATION:

To be considered for this position, please submit a resume and letter of interest to Children of The Woodlands (preschoolbusiness@twmc.org). You can find more information on our website (Children of The Woodlands).
