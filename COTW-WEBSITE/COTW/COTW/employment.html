Employment

2023 Open Positions

Support The Children of The Woodlands (COTW) mission statement, guiding principles and school philosophy as well as The Woodlands Methodist Church (TWMC) mission to reach people for Jesus Christ, disciple them in faith, and help those in need. Provide guidance and expertise to support the COTW Director in the business management of the Preschool. This includes:

ESSENTIAL FUNCTIONS

General Accounting:

  • Month End (Weekly Remittances)
  • Creating next year’s budget
  • Support COTW Director in establishing salary scales
  • Budget vs actuals reporting
  • Manage vendors, sales tax exemptions and W-9s
  • Prepare a 5-year plan for future growth as requested

Employee Set up / Maintenance:

  • New Hire entry into FACTS
  • Resignation & termination paperwork

Student Setup:

  • New tuition input, manage, and reconcile payments including automatic payments
  • Tuition Assistance
  • Withdrawals
  • Financial Policy Updates

General Duties:

  • General Administration duties include carline, front desk coverage, and kitchen upkeep, etc.
  • Greet and assist parents and students as needed
  • Assist with other duties as assigned

QUALIFICATIONS:

  • Exemplify the core values of The Woodlands Methodist Church:
    1. Live Faithfully.
    2. Love Compassionately
    3. Embody Integrity
    4. Offer Encouragement
    5. Remain Teachable
    6. Be Accountable
    7. Multiply Yourself
  • Bachelor’s degree in business or a related field preferred
  • Minimum of 12 college hours of accounting, or related business experience
  • General office experience and good organizational skills
  • Strong interpersonal skills
  • Strong computer skills including a working knowledge of Microsoft Office
  • Must be a committed Christian comfortable sharing their faith with children and families
  • Must meet licensing requirements, which includes fingerprinting and an extensive background check

PHYSICAL DEMANDS:

  • This position requires sitting, standing, lifting, reaching, and crawling.

OTHER SPECIFICATIONS:

  • Reports to: The Children of The Woodlands Director
  • Position Type: Part time, Monday thru Thursday, up to 29 hours per week
  • Schedule: As is typical for a ministry, this position requires a flexible schedule. It includes agreed upon onsite office hours.
  • Compensation: Dependent on experience
  • Benefits: Not benefit eligible

This job description is intended to describe the general nature of this job and may not include all responsibilities that might be required of the person holding this position. This job description is subject to change at any time at the sole discretion of The Children of The Woodlands / The Woodlands Methodist Church and does not establish a contract for employment.

APPLICATION:

Submit cover letter and resume to (employment@twmc.org).

RELATIONAL LEADERSHIP:

  • Support mission statement, guiding principles and school philosophy
  • Provide the best possible learning environment for children assigned to the class, and to work for the best interests of the school
  • Create and maintain a classroom setting that meets accreditation expectations and/or minimum standards
  • Arrive in time to have classroom ready and be prepared for students before devotion time each day
  • Cooperate with Sunday morning teachers and others who share the room
  • Cooperate with the Director of Preschool and other staff in those activities involving the whole program
  • Inform an administrator in cases of unreasonable behavior, parent complaints, or other problems which should be handled by administration in the best interest of the school

STRATEGIC/OPERATIONAL LEADERSHIP:

  • Make daily plans in keeping with the philosophy of the program and the needs of children assigned
  • Assist the classroom teacher following daily plans, working with children, preparing materials, cleaning the room, supervising on the playground, and in keeping with school philosophy
  • Assist in caring for rooms, equipment, and shared space
  • Meet licensing, accreditation and school requirements for in-service training and professional growth in a timely manner
  • Perform other duties as requested
