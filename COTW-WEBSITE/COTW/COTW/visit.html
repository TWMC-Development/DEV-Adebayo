Visit

We invite you to come experience Children of The Woodlands (COTW) by taking a tour on our beautiful campus. Prospective parents and their children will gain valuable insight into the philosophy and future of COTW. Families will have the opportunity to visit classrooms and observe the joyful and engaging learning environment that COTW offers. We are conveniently located in The Woodlands Town Center village between Lake Front Circle and Lake Woodlands Drive off of Grogan’s Mill Road.

We are currently offering tours after school hours to adhere to COVID-19 precautions.

Preschool Tours are scheduled by appointment only. To schedule a tour, please fill out our Information Request Form or call our receptionist at 281-297-5959.
