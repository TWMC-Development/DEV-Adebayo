ONLINE PAYMENTS

Tuition is paid through FACTS, our online payment processor. A tuition plan must be selected during online enrollment. Three plan options are available for tuition payments: one payment, two payments or nine monthly payments. Families that choose monthly installments are assessed a $30 annual fee.
