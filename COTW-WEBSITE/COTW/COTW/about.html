About Our School

Children of The Woodlands is a non-profit ministry of The Woodlands United Methodist Church. Our preschool was the first church-related preschool in the community, founded in 1981. We are dedicated to helping students learn and grow in a loving, stimulating Christian environment. Our purpose is to reflect the highest values while providing the best of care and a quality education for children. Everyday classroom experiences are interpreted in ways consistent with the Bible and Christian values. Students of all faiths are welcome.

Classrooms are filled with hands-on learning experiences, creating a dynamic, Christian learning environment. Low student-to-staff ratios allow us to provide an enriching learning environment that meets the needs of students at his or her developmental level. All preschool classrooms have a teacher and a classroom assistant which allows students many opportunities for individualized and small group learning.
