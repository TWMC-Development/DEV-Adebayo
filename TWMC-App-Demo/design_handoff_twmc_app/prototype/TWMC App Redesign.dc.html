Member App · 2026 Redesign

Same app. New clothes.

A redesign of the current Rock RMS member app onto the 2026 design system — feature-for-feature parity, restyled with the maroon & gold palette, Playfair Display headings, and the warm editorial card language.

What's covered

Every surface in the 14 screenshots: Home, sermon channels & player, pastor articles, all six locations with service times, Tithes & Offerings, and Profile.

Navigation change

The hamburger menu becomes a five-tab bar — Home · Watch · Give · Locations · More. Everything is reachable in one tap; articles live on Home and under More.

Color system

The vivid per-channel colors are remapped to the warm muted palette: Chapel slate, Traditional maroon, Harvest gold, Loft sage — keeping the wayfinding, losing the clash.

Holiness Hidden in Everyday Life

Rev. Mark Sorensen
May 14, 2026 · 4 min read

For the past month, Nycki and I have had the joy of extended time with our grandson, who has been staying at our house while his parents work through the details of relocating back to The Woodlands. Let me just say, having a 3-year-old back in the house after a season of empty nesting has brought a whole new level of awareness into our lives.

A few things my wife and I quickly relearned:

· Silence is no longer comforting; it's suspicious.

· Bath time is actually an experiment to see how much water can escape the tub and still count as a bath.

· A 3-year-old can ask "why?" 17 times in under two minutes.
