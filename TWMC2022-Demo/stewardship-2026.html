A message from Our Senior Pastor

When Life Shakes You, What Spills Out?

Jesus called His followers “the salt of the earth.” Salt brings flavor, preserves what is good, and adds value wherever it’s sprinkled—but it can’t do any of that if it stays locked inside the shaker. Sometimes life shakes us through change, loss, or challenge. But what if the shaking isn’t meant to destroy us, but to deploy us?

In this series, we’ll discover how God uses the shaking of our lives to grow our faith, reveal His treasure within us, and pour out His grace through us into a world that desperately needs it.
