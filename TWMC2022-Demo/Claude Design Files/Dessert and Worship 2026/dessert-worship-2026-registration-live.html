About the Event

Dessert and Worship

Celebrate the arrival of the Christmas season on Thursday, December 3, with an uplifting evening of fellowship, decadent desserts, powerful worship and an inspiring message from author, speaker and Bible study teacher Kristi McLelland.

Meet the speaker

Kristi McLelland

Professor. Teacher. Author.

Kristi is a professor at Williamson College and the bestselling author of Rediscovering Israel, Jesus & Women, Luke in the Land, Feasting on God’s Word and The Gospel on the Ground Bible studies. Kristi teaches the Bible in its historical, cultural, geographic and linguistic contexts. She encourages believers to be postured to receive what the living God is saying through communally experiencing Scripture. Kristi teaches about the goodness of God, often experienced through table fellowship, practicing hospitality and collaborative wisdom.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the date/time of the event?–

Dessert and Worship is on Thursday, December 3. Dessert is at 6:00 p.m., and Worship with a special message from guest Kristi McLelland begins at 7:30 p.m. The event will conclude at 9:00 p.m. with a book signing to follow in the Atrium.

Where do I check in?+

No check-in needed. For any questions, please refer to the information desk in the Atrium.

Do I need a printed ticket?+

No, you do not need a printed ticket. You will receive an email confirmation of your ticket purchase.

How do I find the room and my table number for dessert?+

We will send an email the Monday before the event that includes your room name and table assignment.

Where do I park?+

Parking lots B, C, D, E, F, and G on the east side of the church are closest to the Sanctuary, where Kristi McLelland will be speaking. Simply follow the signage posted in the parking lots.

Where do I go for dessert?+

Dessert takes place throughout our main building, including Wesley Hall, Hayes Hall, Room 204 (A&B) on the second floor, Asbury Hall, Harvest Foyer, Jones Library and Aldersgate Hall for VIPs.

You will receive an email the Monday before the event with your room name and table number. If you have questions the evening of Dessert and Worship, simply stop by the Atrium information desk to gather these details.

Where do I go if I only signed up for the worship and speaker event?+

Kristi McLelland will speak in the Sanctuary. Doors open at 7:00 p.m. Worship begins at 7:30 p.m.

Where do I go if I have a VIP ticket?+

If you have a VIP ticket, please enter Aldersgate Hall, just inside Entrance Four. Please arrive before 6:15 p.m. to be there when Kristi joins the group for a private talk.

What do I get with the VIP ticket?+

VIP attendees are treated to a meet-and-greet with Kristi McLelland, which includes a signed book and a photo op. Kristi will also join the group at 6:15 p.m. to share insights into the evening's talk and answer questions. Additionally, you will have reserved seating in the Sanctuary for the worship and speaker event.

When does the Sanctuary open?+

Doors to the Sanctuary open at 7:00 p.m. Worship and the speaker event begin at 7:30 p.m.

What will Kristi McLelland be talking about?+

We do not review a speaker's topic or content, but typically, they touch on any recently published books with an advent theme.

Will there be a book signing?+

Yes, Kristi McLelland's books will be available for purchase in the Atrium, and there will be a brief book signing period beginning at 9:15 p.m.

Can I purchase tickets the night of the event?+

Yes, you can purchase speaker-only tickets up until the event begins by clicking the register link above.
