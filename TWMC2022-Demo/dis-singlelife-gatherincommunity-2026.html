Tea Around Town

All Singles

Local

Thursday, May 7, 2026
Depart TWMC Aldersgate Entrance (Entrance 4) at 10:45 a.m.
Tea Around Town
500 McKinney, Houston, Texas →
No child care provided
$95.00 per person

This is a great way to kick off Mother's Day weekend! Experience Tea Around Town in Houston, where afternoon tea meets big-city energy. Step aboard their signature double-decker, pink buys and enjoy fine teas, sweet and savory bites, and lively entertainment. From Discovery Green to the Theater District and the iconic skyline, every moment combines Houston's vibrant spirit with a festive ride to remember. This is a beautiful day with friends or family!

Magnolia Market Trip

All Singles

Day Trip

Saturday, June 13, 2026
Depart TWMC south entrance (by Robb Chapel) at 6:30 a.m.
Magnolia Market
601 Webster Ave, Waco, Texas →
No child care provided
$30.00 per person for transportation only. Lunch and all purchases are the responsibility of each individual.

Join us on our road trip to Waco! We will make the obligatory stop at Buc-ee's in Madisonville, explore Magnolia Market and the surrounding area, locate a few houses renovated by Chip and Joanna Gaines, touring the cute town of McGregor where the Magnolia Bed and Breakfast is located before heading back home.

This will be a day trip in a 15-passenger van, so space is limited.
