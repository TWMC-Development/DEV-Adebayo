Christmas is a time of celebration, a season to share love and joy with those closest to us. We exchange gifts and acts of kindness, honoring the greatest gift ever given: a baby named Jesus, the Savior of the world.

At His birth, He was called Emmanuel, meaning “God with us,” the gift and promise of His constant presence in our lives.

This Christmas, we invite you to join us at The Woodlands Methodist Church as we celebrate the joy of the season. Together, let’s make cherished memories with family and friends as we honor our risen King — Christ Jesus, Emmanuel!

SERVICIOS EN ESPAÑOL

Redescubra este maravilloso tiempo de Navidad en el que Dios se hizo hombre y quiso vivir entre nosotros y con nosotros. El Servicio de Navidad del Pacto es una oportunidad para conectarnos con Dios mientras cantamos villancicos, leemos las Escrituras y escuchamos un mensaje navideño. Ofrecemos opciones de cuidado infantil para niños menores de 10 años o, si lo prefieres, tus hijos pueden quedarse contigo durante el culto. También hay una sala de llanto justo al lado del santuario.

Después del servicio navideño, disfrute de una obra navideña familiar de 30 minutos.

Angel Tree

November 16-30

The Woodlands Campus

Angel Tree, a ministry of Prison Fellowship, provides a pathway for moms and dads in prison to strengthen the relationship with their children through the giving of Christmas gifts. Help make Christmas special for these families by purchasing toys and clothing for these children. Gift tags for children will be available for pickup before and after services, November 16-30. Find more information at twmc.org/outward

Christmas Miracles

November 16 ‐ December 7

The Woodlands Campus, Woodforest Campus

Creekside Campus
