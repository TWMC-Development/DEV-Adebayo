Our Core Values

At The Woodlands Methodist Church, our staff are more than teammates — they are ministry partners who embody the heart of our mission: Reach People for Jesus. Disciple Them In Faith. Help Those in Need.

Each year, we celebrate those who live out our Core Values in extraordinary ways through our Core Value Awards. These values define what it means to serve Christ faithfully, love people well, and lead with integrity.

Live Faithfully

We trust God in all things and walk in obedience to His Word. Faithfulness means showing up every day with a heart ready to serve — not for recognition, but because we believe God is at work in every task, big or small.

Love Compassionately

Jesus loved people deeply and personally. We follow His example by extending grace, kindness, and understanding to those we serve and those we serve alongside. Compassion fuels every ministry moment.

Embody Integrity

We strive to do the right thing — even when no one is watching. Integrity builds trust among our team, our congregation, and our community, reflecting the character of Christ in all we do.

Offer Encouragement

Words have power. We speak life into one another, celebrating wins, lifting spirits, and inspiring courage in times of challenge. Encouragement is the spark that keeps our mission joyful and united.

Remain Teachable

We never stop learning — from God, from one another, and from the people we serve. A teachable spirit keeps us humble, curious, and ready for the new things God wants to do through us.

Be Accountable

We hold ourselves and each other to high standards of excellence and grace. Accountability is not about perfection — it’s about ownership, honesty, and growing stronger together.

Multiply Yourself

We lead by raising others to lead. When we invest in others’ growth, we multiply impact far beyond ourselves — ensuring that Christ’s work continues in new hands, new hearts, and new generations.

Why These Values Matter

These values aren’t just words on a wall — they are lived out every day through the dedication of our staff. The Core Value Awards honor those who shine as examples of what it looks like to serve Christ wholeheartedly.

Your gift to the Staff Gift Program helps us celebrate their faithfulness and bless every member of our team who gives so much throughout the year.
