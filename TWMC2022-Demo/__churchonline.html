Church Online offers an interactive, accessible way to engage in church through live chat, prayer requests, note-taking, and Bible integration, serving as a meaningful complement to in-person worship.

Choose Your Service

TRADITIONAL

Sundays at 7:45 a.m. (Chapel) and 11:00 a.m. (Sanctuary)

We offer an inspirational, Biblical message combined with your favorite classic hymns to create a beautiful traditional worship service. Add in a large choir and full orchestra and you’ll leave the morning feeling uplifted and inspired.

Watch Online

HARVEST

Sundays 9:00 a.m (Sanctuary)

You’ll be moved by this contemporary worship service. Our pastor specializes in encouraging Bible teaching to give you the lift you need to face the week. Prayer and a worshipful praise team add to this inspiring service.

Watch Online

LOFT

Sundays at 9:30 and 11:00 a.m. (Loft Worship Center)

Looking for a place to ask questions and explore life with Jesus? Our service features relevant teaching about faith and culture with energetic music and innovative media. At the Loft, your past doesn’t matter; you’re welcome here.

Watch Online

WOODFOREST

Sundays at 9:00 (Woodforest Worship Center)

You’re invited to worship with this “front porch” community of believers at the Church at Woodforest. We love to share our uplifting worship music and engaging Bible teaching with families and individuals of all ages. Join us on Sunday morning and be inspired for the rest of your week.
