About the Program

What is Confirmation?

Confirmation is a structured, year-long program that equips students with a strong biblical foundation, encourages spiritual growth and prepares them to take ownership of their faith as members of The Woodlands Methodist Church.

Catechesis
A year of religious instruction in the core of Christian faith.
Laying on of Hands
A blessing from TWMC pastors on Confirmation Sunday in May.
Holy Spirit
A prayer for the Spirit to move in the heart of each confirmand.
The Journey

A curriculum in three movements

Across the school year, students move through three guided seasons — building roots in Scripture, growing in personal faith, and learning to thrive as disciples.

Phase 01

Roots

Aug — Nov

Build a foundation in Scripture and God’s story.

Phase 02

Grow

Dec — Feb

Develop personal faith, discipleship and worship.

Phase 03

Thrive

Mar — May

Apply faith through service and mission.

Questions, Answered

Confirmation FAQ

What is a house group?

A small group of 7–10 students led by parents and student leaders for growth and accountability.

What happens each week?

Teaching, small group discussion and interactive activities.

What if my student misses a session?

Consistent participation is important. Occasional absences should be communicated, and students are responsible for staying on track.

What are the required commitments?

  • Weekly session attendance
  • Regular worship participation
  • Confirmation Retreat attendance
  • Scripture memorization
  • Service projects
  • Group Bible study involvement

How much time should we expect outside of Sundays?

Students will need time for Scripture memorization, service projects and occasional group activities.

Is the retreat mandatory?

Yes. The Confirmation Retreat is a required part of the program.

When is Confirmation Sunday?

Confirmation takes place in May at the conclusion of the program year.

Who leads my student’s group?

  • Parent leaders
  • High school student leaders

How are groups formed?

Students are placed in small groups of 7–10 to encourage connection and accountability.

What will my student learn?

  • Study Scripture
  • Develop personal faith practices
  • Learn to live out their faith through service

What is my role as a parent?

  • Support consistent attendance
  • Reinforce spiritual conversations at home
  • Encourage participation in all requirements

What does it mean to complete Confirmation?

Students who fulfill all requirements are prepared for full membership at TWMC.

What happens after Confirmation?

Students are encouraged to continue in worship, discipleship and service within the church.
