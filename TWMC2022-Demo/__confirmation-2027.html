About the Program

What is Confirmation?

Confirmation is a structured program that guides students through essential Christian teachings, fosters spiritual maturity, and helps them confidently embrace their identity as members of The Woodlands Methodist Church.

Catechesis
Systematic teaching of faith, Scripture and the Christian doctrine
Laying on of Hands
A blessing from TWMC pastors on Confirmation Sunday in May
Holy Spirit
A prayer for the Holy Spirit to work in the heart of each confirmand
The Journey

A curriculum in three phases

Across the school year, students move through three guided phases: building roots in Scripture, growing in personal faith, and learning to thrive as disciples.

Phase 01

Roots

Aug. — Nov.

Build a foundation in God’s Word and the story of Scripture.

Phase 02

Grow

Dec. — Feb.

Develop habits of personal faith, discipleship and worship.

Phase 03

Thrive

Mar. — May.

Put faith into action through service and mission.
