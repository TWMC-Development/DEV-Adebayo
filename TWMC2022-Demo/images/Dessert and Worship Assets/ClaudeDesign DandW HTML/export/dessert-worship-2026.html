Meet the speaker

Kristi McLelland

Speaker · Author · Teacher · College Professor

Since completing her Masters of Arts in Christian Education at Dallas Theological Seminary, she has dedicated her life to discipleship, to teaching people how to study the Bible for themselves, and to writing about how God is better than we ever knew by explaining the Bible through a Middle Eastern lens.

Her great desire for people to truly experience the love of God birthed a ministry in which she leads biblical study trips to Israel, Turkey, Greece, and Italy.

Good to know

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the date/time of the event?–

Dessert and Worship is on Thursday, December 3. Dessert is at 6:00 p.m., and Worship with a special message from guest Kristi McLelland begins at 7:30 p.m. The event will conclude at 9:00 p.m. with a book signing to follow in the Atrium.

Where do I check in?+

No check in needed. For any questions, please refer to the information desk in the Atrium.

Do I need a ticket?+

No, you do not need a ticket for the dessert and speaker event portions of the evening.

How do I find the room and my table number for dessert?+

We will send an email the Monday before the event that includes your room name and table assignment.

Where do I park?+

Parking lots B, C, D, E, F, and G on the east side of the church are closest to the Sanctuary, where Kristi McLelland will be speaking. Simply follow the signage posted in the parking lots.

Where do I go for Dessert?+

Dessert takes place throughout our main building, including Wesley Hall, Hayes Hall, Room 204 (A&B) on the second floor, Asbury Hall, Harvest Foyer and Jones Library and Aldersgate Hall (VIP).

You will receive an email the Monday before the event with your room name and table number. If you have questions the night of, simply stop by the Atrium information desk the evening of Dessert and Worship to gather these details.

Where do I go if I only signed up for the Worship and speaker event?+

Kristi McLelland will speak in the Sanctuary. Doors open at 7:00 p.m. Worship begins at 7:30 p.m.

Where do I go if I have a VIP ticket?+

If you have a VIP ticket, please enter Aldersgate Hall, just inside Entrance Four. Please arrive before 6:15 pm to be there when Kristi joins the group for a private talk.

What do I get with the VIP ticket?+

VIP attendees receive a meet-and-greet with Kristi McLelland, which includes a signed book and a photo op. Kristi will also join the group at 6:15 p.m.to share insights into the evening's talk and answer questions. Additionally, you will have reserved seating in the Sanctuary for the Worship and speaker event.

When does the Sanctuary open?+

Doors to the Sanctuary open at 7:00 p.m. Worship and the speaker event begin at 7:30 p.m.

What will Kristi McLelland be talking about?+

We do not review a speaker's topic or content, but typically, they touch on any recently published books with an advent theme.

Will there be a book signing?+

Yes, Kristi McLelland's books will be available for purchase in the Atrium, and there will be a brief book signing period beginning at 9:15 p.m.

Can I purchase tickets the night of the event?+

Yes, you can purchase speaker only tickets up until the event begins by clicking the register link above.
