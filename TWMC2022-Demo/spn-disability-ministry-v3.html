Ministry for all ages and abilities

WELCOME!

Disability Ministry’s heart is to make Jesus accessible across all ages and abilities. We see each person as beautifully and wonderfully created in the image of God capable of spiritual experience and belonging. Whether 5 or 55, we encourage and equip individuals and their families to grow closer to God, confident in their value, and connected in community.

From Special Needs Family Worship to Sunday School, inclusive and adapted children’s events to youth and adult activities like prom and Fun Friday, Deaf Gatherings and ASL interpretation in worship, there are a variety of opportunities for respite, relationship, and holy renewal.

FAMILY WORSHIP

Sunday, Month Date, Year | 11:00 a.m. | Wesley Hall

Led by individuals with special needs, Family Worship is a joyful experience of the presence of God! It is safe and inclusive space for families to worship together and enjoy a meal afterward. We support families with fidgets, headphones, sensory and calming tools. It is ok to move or make noise!
We will praise God together every 5th Sunday and for a special service during the Christmas season.
