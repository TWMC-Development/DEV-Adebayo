NextGen Students With Special Needs

REMIX – Young Adults

REMIX serves teens with special needs from 7th grade through high school graduation. We offer Sunday morning programs as well as other social and recreational opportunities. Our desire is to share the love of Christ with your student and help them build Godly relationships that will last a lifetime.

We offer Sunday school at 9:30 and 11:00 a.m. in the Loft Building, Room LN103. Students can mainstream into the 11:00 a.m. NextGen Students program or attend one of our sensory-friendly classes at either 9:30 or 11:00 a.m. with developmentally appropriate Bible lessons. Special Needs Ministry volunteers and interns are assigned to assist students in either program.

NextGen Students Creekside Campus

Stay Connected to Everything NextGen Students

Being a teen in today’s world is tough. Parenting a teen in today’s world can be overwhelming. We’re happy to discuss with you any special concerns you have regarding your junior high or high school student. We’re eager to support you as you and your child navigate this unique life stage.

Wednesday Bible Study

Wednesdays during the school year | 6:00 – 7:30 p.m.
6th – 12th grade

We welcome all students in junior high school and high school to enjoy fellowship with NextGen Students. We meet at the church for about 45 minutes of outdoor-based activity before jumping into some Bible study for the second half of our evenings.

Sunday Worship

Recent trends suggest that many teenagers lose connection with the church as they transition into adulthood. These are often individuals who have been active in “kids church” and student programs but later perceive church as irrelevant. What’s causing this disconnection? How can we foster continued engagement among young adults? We believe the key lies in promoting multigenerational relationships.

At Covenant Church at Creekside, we prioritize intergenerational worship; therefore, we don’t offer separate services specifically for students. We believe it’s vital for teens to worship alongside and build relationships with individuals of all ages within our church community.

By instilling a sense of belonging within a church family, we aim to equip our students with the foundation for healthy relationships and a lasting faith journey into adulthood.

Meet Our Leadership

Arnold Arreaga
NextGen Students Shepherd
Arnold is honored to serve as NextGen Students Shepherd for Covenant Church at Creekside. Born and raised in Guatemala, he moved to Houston in 2019, answering God’s call to ministry. His journey began as a worship leader, using music to share the gospel, but God led him to youth and communications ministry — shaping him for this moment. Through these experiences, he learned to serve with love, empowering the next generation to live boldly for Christ. Arnold is passionate about walking alongside students and families, helping them discover their God-given gifts, and inspiring them to make an impact for His kingdom.
