The Apostle John invites us in Revelation to behold our beautiful God. It is in our beholding of God that we discover the beauty of our Belovedness and the true intimacy and strength of prayer and worship. As Christ’s followers, we often focus our prayer and worship on ourselves – our style, our posture and our words. But the Apostle John reminds us in the Book of Revelation that prayer and worship begin with discovering the beauty and radiance of our God. Only then can we understand our true belovedness and our equipping in the battle with self and the world around us. Come see who He is and who we are in Him. This is the place where worship arises and prayer begins.

Allen Hood

Allen Hood (M.Div., Asbury Theological Seminary) served as the Associate Director of a large missions base from 2000 to 2020, overseeing more than 500 full-time staff, and was the President of its Bible College from 2003 to 2016.

In December 2019, Allen founded the Excellencies of Christ Ministries, which exists to proclaim the excellencies of Christ to the ends of the earth, disciple the Church in the knowledge of God through courses, books, schools, media, and creative resources, and build communities of love for Jesus that contend in prayer for revival in the Church and awakening in society.

Allen lives in Port Saint Lucie, FL, with his wife, Rachel. He regularly travels, ministers, and consults with various ministries, local churches, and houses of prayer worldwide.
