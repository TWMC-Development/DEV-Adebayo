Invest in What Matters Most

Every marriage is a journey, with seasons of joy, growth and challenge. Whether your marriage is thriving and you want to make it even better, you're navigating a difficult season, or you find yourselves somewhere in between, re|engage is a place for you.

This is more than just a class; it's a community dedicated to helping you and your spouse grow closer to each other and to God. We believe that every marriage can be strengthened by applying biblical principles in a supportive, authentic environment.

What to expect?

What to expect?

A Safe Place to Grow Together

Re|engage is a marriage enrichment program that offers a safe space for couples to connect and learn. Here, you will find:

  • Biblical Guidance: Discover timeless truths from scripture that provide a blueprint for a healthy, Christ-centered marriage.
  • Authentic Community: Connect with other couples who are on the same journey, sharing experiences and encouragement along the way.
  • Guided Growth: Through a mix of teaching, small group discussions and personal stories, you'll gain practical tools to strengthen your marital bond and deepen your faith.

This program is for any married couple, at any stage. You will find a welcoming atmosphere where you can be real with each other and find harmony and resolution.

Your Journey to a Stronger Marriage Starts Here

Are you ready to invest in your relationship and build a marriage that reflects God's love? Join us for re|engage and take the next step in your journey together. Let's build something beautiful and lasting, rooted in faith and community.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the program last?

The program runs for 15 weeks, excluding the week of Spring Break. It begins with an Open House orientation, followed by 14 weekly sessions with your small group.

When is the re|engage Open House?

The Open House is on Tuesday, January 13, 2026, at 6:00 p.m. in Wesley Hall. in Wesley Hall. Couples who wish to join a small group for the upcoming session must attend.

What happens at the Open House?

The Open House is an orientation session where you will learn more about the program and receive your small group assignment. It's the first step in your re|engage journey.

When do the small groups start meeting?

Small groups begin January 20 at The Woodlands Campus and January 27 at the Woodforest Campus.

What does the re|engage program involve?

Couples work through a 14-lesson curriculum designed to strengthen their relationship with each other and with God. The program takes place in a small group setting, with four to six couples and a facilitator couple who guides the discussion.

What is the attendance expectation?

Consistent attendance is one of the most important keys to getting the most out of re|engage. We understand that life happens, but if you expect to miss more than three sessions, please {[ emailform email:'ajones@twmc.org' contactname:'contact Amy Jones' class:’a-reengage2’ ]} to discuss your situation.

Is childcare provided?

Yes, childcare is available on a limited basis. We encourage you to register early to reserve a spot for your children.

Are there dinner options available?

Yes, for those attending at The Woodlands Campus, dinner is available for purchase starting Tuesday, January 20.

  • Time & Location: 6:00 p.m. in Wesley Hall
  • Cost: $12 for adult meals and $5 for kids’ meals
  • Note: Your first meal and the Valentine's meal are complimentary! Weekly registration links will be sent out so you can order dinner in advance.
