FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is Forge?

Forge is a weekend retreat for junior high and high school students, happening February 13–14, 2026, at our The Woodlands campus . It’s designed to ignite faith, build friendships, and grow closer to God through worship, teaching, and fun activities.

Who can attend?

All students in grades 7–12 are welcome.

Can out-of-town churches attend?

Yes! We’d love to have you. Please email {[ emailform email:'forge@twmc.org' contactname:'forge@twmc.org' class:`a-traditional`]} with any questions or to get more information about the weekend.

What are the dates and times?

Forge takes place February 13–14, 2026. Check-in begins Friday at 6:00 p.m., and concludes on Saturday night.

How do I register?

You can register online!

  • Student registration
  • Group registration

Early registration is encouraged, as spots are limited!

What does it cost?

The cost is $150 per student, which includes breakfast and lunch on Saturday, all activities, and a T-shirt.

Is there a registration deadline?

Yes — registration closes on January 25.

Are scholarships available?

Yes. We never want cost to be a barrier. Email {[ emailform email:'kkemp@twmc.org' contactname:'Contact Karen Kemp' class:’a-traditional’ ]} for our scholarship application.

Where do students stay overnight?

Local students will return to their own homes for the night. If you're joining us from another city or state, we recommend checking VRBO for cost-effective group accommodations — it’s typically the most affordable option in The Woodlands.

There are also several hotels near the TWMC campus, and we’re happy to help secure a discounted rate for your group. If you need assistance with booking, feel free to contact us at {[ emailform email:'forge@twmc.org' contactname:'forge@twmc.org' class:’a-traditional’ ]}

What about meals and allergies?

We’ll provide breakfast and lunch on Saturday, along with snacks on both Friday and Saturday. Please list any allergies or dietary needs during registration so we can do our best to accommodate.

Who are the leaders and volunteers?

All leaders and volunteers are background-checked and trained college students or young adults to ensure a safe, Christ-centered environment. We’ll also have some parent volunteers serving throughout the weekend!

What if my student needs medication?

Please list any medications on the registration form and check in any necessary items with our medical team at check-in.

What will students do at Forge?

Students will experience powerful worship, dynamic speakers, small group discussions, and fun team activities — all designed to deepen their faith and build lasting friendships.

Who are the speakers and worship leaders?

We’ll be announcing them soon — stay tuned!

Would you like to lead a small group?

We would love to have you! Email {[ emailform email:'forge@twmc.org' contactname:'forge@twmc.org' class:`a-traditional`]} for more information and note it in the registration link.

Will there be free time?

Yes! The schedule includes a good balance of sessions, meals, and recreation time, giving students the chance to relax, hang out with friends, and connect with their leaders.

What should students bring?

Students should bring a Bible and a notebook for taking notes during sessions.

Can parents volunteer?

Absolutely! We welcome parent volunteers to help with check-in, hospitality, prayer support, and even as small group leaders. You can sign up during registration.

What is the cancellation/refund policy?

Cancellations made by January 25 will receive a full refund.
