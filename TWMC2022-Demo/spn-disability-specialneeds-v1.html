VOLUNTEER WITH US!

We are looking for volunteers to companion youth and adults with special needs. Be a part of experiencing the love of Jesus through genuine presence and shared discipleship. You can be a friend during Sunday School, help organize events like the SNYA Prom, or encourage SNYA parents, there are many possibilities for God's love to shine through you!

We provide training, guidance, and a supportive team environment to ensure you feel confident and equipped.

Come be a part of something truly special. Whether you can commit weekly or occasionally, your presence can make a big impact.
