The Woodlands Campus

Sunday School

We want to reach and teach your children in such a way that they have the greatest opportunity to become devoted followers of Jesus. In NextGen Kids Sunday School, your kids will worship, learn and have fun at their own level.

Sunday School Times

Main Building

Sundays | 9:00 and 11:00 a.m.

FLC Building

Saturdays | 6:00 p.m.
Sundays | 9:00 and 11:00 a.m.

Meeting Locations

Main Building:

  • 3 years – Kindergarten: Downstairs in the rooms behind the big fish tank
  • 1st – 4th Grade: NextGen Kids space upstairs by the yellow slide
  • 5th – 6th Grade: Family Life Center across from Main Building

FLC Building:

  • 3 years – Kindergarten: Early Childhood Large Group Space
  • 1st – 4th Grade: Elementary Large Group Space

Check In

Parents check their young children in on the computers close to their meeting areas
Older children can check themselves in at the computers close to their meeting areas

Meeting Format

Each age group meets in a large group for worship and the main lesson and then they break up into small groups for age-appropriate discussion and activities.

Children with Special Needs

We have Sunday School classes for children with special needs in preschool through 6th grade on the Main Campus and in the Loft.

Nursery (6 weeks - 12 years)

Children are precious to us at The Woodlands Methodist Church. We strive to make our Noah’s Nook Nursery fun and stimulating for even the youngest child coming through our door. While we realize it can be difficult to leave your child in the care of others, we want to assure you that our staff has a genuine love for the work they do.

Our Nursery is staffed by paid, experienced workers who are trained in the areas of child care, child development, safety and sanitation and certified by the American Heart Association in CPR and First Aid.

We offer a loving, Christian atmosphere. All of our rooms are safe, fun and filled with age-appropriate toys, games and books.

Child Care Reservations

Worship Services

Please refer to Re-Opening information for the most current details on Children's programming and child care options.

Reservations are not necessary for worship services. During worship services, all are welcome to bring children to the Nursery, infants through two years old (as of September 1).

Bible Studies, Programs and Events

Reservations are needed for Nursery care during all programs, except worship services. The Ministry leader sponsoring your Bible Study, program or event is responsible for requesting Nursery care and taking reservations. Reservations may be made online or through a ministry contact person. The Nursery cares for children, infants through age 12.

Nursery Cost

Nursery care is provided at no cost for worship services and many church-related programs and events. However, Nursery care for some events may require a fee. For more information on nursery reservations please call 281.297.5949

Early Childhood (3, Pre-K, and Kindergarten)

Our main program includes dozens of weekly volunteers to help support a small group of children. A core Early Childhood staff and interns help to organize, prepare and set up lessons and activities each week. Our young children participate in large group praise and worship where they sing songs and hear about Jesus. Volunteers oversee small groups in age specific classrooms in order to focus on the biblical truths that our children are learning. Children in our Sunday school program must be 3 years old by September 1 of the current school year.

Creekside Campus

Sunday School

NextGen COVkids at Covenant Church at Creekside is a safe, welcoming place for children in kindergarten through fifth grade to learn, explore and discover who they are in Christ. As valued members of our church family, kids are encouraged to build friendships across generations, experience meaningful community and grow in their faith.

During NextGen Kids Sunday School, children worship, learn and have fun in age-appropriate environments designed just for them. Classes are offered during Sunday worship at 9:00, 11:00 a.m. and 1:00 p.m.

Throughout the year, we also host special monthly activities and studies that help kids deepen their faith and build lasting friendships.

Sunday School Times

9:00 and 11:00 a.m.

Meeting Locations

  • 3 – 5 years old: Room 4
  • Kindergarten – 5th Grade: The Cove

Check In

Check-in area is located just outside the main entrance. NextGen staff will meet your family, check your child into the system and walk you through how NextGen Kids works.

3 – 5 Years Old

  • Parents check in their young children to receive a printed name tag and a security tag.
  • Parents place name tag on child’s back, keep security tag for pick up and escort child to classroom.
  • Upon pick up, parents will show security tag to the child care worker.

Kindergarten – 5th Grade

  • Older children start in worship with their families and will be dismissed to NextGen Kids during the worship service.
  • Adult helpers will walk them to The Cove for assembly and class time.
  • Children will need to be collected from their respective classes after service. K-2: Room 2 and 3-5: Room 3

WHAT TO EXPECT

3 – 5 Years Old
Our staff with 3 – 5 year olds are background checked and Safe Sanctuary trained. They help little ones learn about God’s Word at their own level. The stories read each week correspond to those learned by the older children and are accompanied by fun crafts and activities.

Kindergarten – 5th Grade
We believe it’s important for kids to gradually learn to participate in “big” worship. During the sermon time, elementary kids will be dismissed and escorted to The Cove for a special time of fun, discovery, learning and worship. There they will gather with other students their age so they can learn about God’s Word at their own pace and level. We will partkae in Holy Communion the first Sunday of the month.

Nursery (6 weeks – 2 Years Old)

On Sundays, loving nursery care is available throughout the entire service for infants and toddlers. Our nursery staff are consistent, experienced, Safe Sanctuary trained and background checked, so you can be sure that your little ones are in excellent hands.

Children are precious to us at Covenant Church at Creekside. We strive to make our nursery fun and stimulating for even the youngest child coming through our door. While we realize it can be difficult to leave your child in the care of others, we want to assure you that our staff has a genuine love for the work they do.

We offer a loving, Christian atmosphere. Our rooms are safe, fun and filled with age-appropriate toys, games and books.

Child Care Reservations

Reservations are not necessary for worship services. Nursery care is provided at no cost for worship services.

Wednesday Night Bible Study

NextGen COVkids exists to share the love of Jesus with children in a safe, joyful and welcoming environment. Our seasonal Wednesday Night Bible Studies help kids deepen their faith through engaging Bible stories, worship, prayer and hands-on activities. Along the way, they build meaningful friendships and learn to live out kindness, compassion and Christ-centered values.

Sessions are offered throughout the year and require registration. We are honored to partner with families to build a strong foundation of faith rooted in God’s love.

Montgomery Campus

About CAM Kids

CAM Kids serves children from kindergarten through fifth grade. We provide children’s ministry programming during worship services and host special events throughout the year to engage families and the surrounding community.

CAM Kids exists to share the love of Jesus with children in a safe, joyful and welcoming environment. Through Bible stories, worship, prayer and hands-on activities, we help children grow in their faith while learning Christian values such as kindness, compassion and love for others. We partner with families to build a strong foundation of faith rooted in Christ and meaningful relationships within the church.
