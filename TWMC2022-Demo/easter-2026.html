COME AND BE FILLED THIS EASTER

Just as our hearts seek what truly satisfies, Easter reveals the resurrection of Jesus as the source of enduring hope, renewing love and new life.

Deep down, we all carry a holy hunger — a longing for a hope that endures and a love that never fails. Just as spring follows winter, our spirits crave the renewal found in the resurrection. The empty tomb is a promise that our deepest needs are fully met through our faith in Jesus.

This Easter, rediscover hope at The Woodlands Methodist Church. We offer contemporary, traditional, modern, Spanish-speaking, and ASL-translated worship services for the whole family. Join us across our four campuses as we celebrate the Savior who fills us with new life.

Easter Services

This Easter, rediscover hope at The Woodlands Methodist Church. We offer contemporary, traditional, modern, Spanish-speaking and ASL-translated worship services for the whole family. Join us across our four campuses as we celebrate the Savior who fills us with new life.

Nursery available for children 2 and under at all services except Sunrise Worship. Children (age 3 through 4th grade) and students (5th through 12th grade) will attend Easter services with their families. All NextGen Ministries programming will resume April 12.

In case of inclement weather, outdoor services will be relocated. Any location updates will be made on the website.
