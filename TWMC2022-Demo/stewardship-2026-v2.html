At the beginning of each year, The Woodlands Methodist Church emphasizes the value of stewardship, encouraging generosity and the annual financial commitments of its members to support the vital work of its ministries.

We believe stewardship is the faithful response to life’s uncertainties. It is the understanding that everything we have — our time, resources, talents, and even our struggles — belongs to God and can be used for His purpose.

When life shakes us, stewardship is the act of holding loosely to what is temporary so we can cling tightly to what is eternal. It means trusting that God does not shake us to break us, but to shape us into vessels of His love. By pouring ourselves out for others, we participate in His mission, ensuring that what spills out of our lives is generosity, hope, and an unshakable faith.

God Shakes to Activate Us

“You are the salt of the earth. But if the salt loses its saltiness, how can it be made salty again? It is no longer good for anything, except to be thrown out and trampled underfoot.

— Matthew 5:13

Subtítulos en español disponibles

We are children of God, and just like any parent He has an endless love for us. When He calls us “salt of the earth” He is declaring value and purpose in us, but living into that purpose doesn’t always come easy. It’s through life’s challenges we hope to grow and be refined. To find a meaning that can only be found in God. We hold the choice to trust in God when we feel shaken. To trust that He will provide. And when we trust, our faith pours out. It overflows unexpectedly in an everyday conversation with a neighbor or co-worker, or in comforting a family member. When we trust God in the shaking, we find that His love pours out of us and our faith pours into another.

Pray this prayer with me. God I offer myself to your will; your purpose for me. Forgive me for the times I have not placed my trust in you. I ask today that you be the light on my path so that I can be the salt poured our wherever I go. Amen.

God allows us to be broken to reveal hidden treasure

But we have this treasure in jars of clay to show that this all-surpassing power is from God and not from us. We are hard pressed on every side, but not crushed; perplexed, but not in despair; persecuted, but not abandoned; struck down, but not destroyed.

— 2 Corinthians 4:7-9

Subtítulos en español disponibles

Each of our lives is a gift from God. We live because of His creation and ultimately His sacrifice of His son, Jesus Christ. In every way, we live because of God. Compared to our human experience, can you imagine the power and strength of God? To give so generously and without end? We are challenged in so many ways. We can feel defeated, broken, even hopeless, but God tells us “we have this treasure in jars of clay”; meaning that the treasure of His power, love and sacrifice is already within us. Even when we feel cracks growing in our life, when we feel shattered, it’s a chance for the treasure, the gift of Jesus within us, to shine through.

Will you pray with me. Amen.

God Pours Us Out to Bless Others

But I will rejoice even if I lose my life, pouring it out like a liquid offering to God, just like your faithful service is an offering to God.

— Philippians 2:17a (NLT)

Subtítulos en español disponibles

Everyday, we fill our minds and our schedules with the responsibilities we take on. From doing homework to making dinner most of us can’t wait to finish the task at hand and take a breath. What of these things is done in service to God? With all He has given us what are we giving back to Him? Acknowledging the kingdom work needed all around us and stepping into a willingness to join in is also stepping into stewardship. Checking chores off the list is an act of service to our home and family. We give of ourselves out of care for self or another. In the bible, Paul and Timothy wrote to all followers of Jesus, “even if I lose my life, pouring it out like a liquid offering to God, just like your faithful service is an offering to God.” This scripture declares that the entirety of our life, in all its fullness, can be an act of devotion to God. How would your life change if you prioritized living in honor and service to God above all else? When we give of what God has blessed us with, whether our time, service, or treasures; we overflow in our faith, we find a maturing in our spirit and we are poured back into in our obedience to God. Remember this one simple truth: we can never outgive God.

God Anchors Us in What Cannot Be Shaken

Therefore, since we are receiving a kingdom that cannot be shaken, let us be thankful, and so worship God acceptably with reverence and awe.

— Hebrews 12:28

Subtítulos en español disponibles

Even though the world shakes around us and we feel the rumbling in fragile economies, strained relationships, unexpected storms, there is still good news. Because of our faith in Jesus, we stand on an unshakable foundation. When we build our lives and invest our time, talents, and treasure in what God’s Word says will last forever, we are anchoring ourselves in that eternal kingdom that will never be shaken.

Because of this truth, we build our lives on what God’s Word says will last forever, and we invest our time, our talents, and our treasure in His kingdom, and in doing so we anchor ourselves in something greater than our own abilities, our own desires, and our own strength. This kind of unshakable faith doesn’t just secure our own hearts. It shines as a testimony to the world. People notice when God’s perfect love is real in us, especially when our trust in Him doesn’t waver, even in hard seasons.

The greatest call on our lives is to live with unshakable faith in a shaking world. Our steadfast trust in God doesn’t just benefit us—it becomes a visible testimony of His perfect love through Jesus. Others see that God is faithful, His promises true, and His love unwavering.

Stewarding our entire lives in this way—our hearts, our resources, our relationships—is the ultimate act of discipleship and stewardship. It’s not about holding on tightly to what we have but choosing to trust God fully, even with the ground feels shaky, and letting Him multiply our faith for His kingdom and for His glory.

What if I’m already giving?

We are so grateful for your ongoing generosity and commitment to our church community. Even if you have already set up an automatic giving schedule, we invite you to share your annual intent to give by making a stewardship pledge. This step helps us thoughtfully plan for ministry needs and continue offering programs and events that nurture our congregation and serve the wider community. Please remember, a stewardship pledge reflects contributions to the general fund of the church and does not include other forms of giving, such as missions. Thank you for being a vital part of this meaningful work!

How can I participate in stewardship?

You can participate by making an annual commitment pledge and finding meaningful ways to honor God in your daily life. While we believe in the biblical tithe of 10% given to God, generosity can begin with any amount. Additionally, you can give of your time by volunteering on a serve team or participating in local missions projects. Together, we can make a difference and live out our faith through both giving and serving.

Does stewardship focus only on financial giving

Not at all. Stewardship is about the holistic act of surrendering everything we have to God. While financial giving is part of it, stewardship also involves embracing who God made us to be and offering our time and talents to do His work on earth. These are all part of living a generous life of stewardship.

How does my giving leave a legacy?

Your contributions support ministries and programs that impact future generations, spread God’s Word and meet tangible needs in our church and beyond. You’re investing in eternity. Join us in making room for His work in our lives and in our community.
