CCM Training Program

Comprehensive Preparation for Ministry

Our Congregational Care Ministry (CCM) training is more than a class—it's a transformative experience that prepares you for meaningful, Christ-centered ministry with seniors. The curriculum includes:

Biblical foundation for congregational care

Effective listening and communication skills

Understanding aging and senior needs

Boundaries and self-care for volunteers

Safety protocols and best practices
