The Treehouse Indoor Playground

The Treehouse Indoor Playground is now open! A canopy of fun awaits, giving children a safe space to learn, play and build lasting friendships, even on rainy days.

Hours

Monday - Friday | Noon – 6:00 p.m.
Saturday | 9:00 a.m. - 6:00 p.m.
Sunday | Noon – 6:00 p.m.

Location

The Treehouse is located across from the Fireside Cafe at the north end of the main building.
Get directions →

Rules for Play

  • Children must be supervised by an adult at all times.
  • Playscape is designed for children ages 4–10.
  • Please remove shoes, loose clothing, and jewelry before using the play equipment.
  • Adults should use caution when using play equipment.
  • Slide one at a time in a forward seated position, with your feet first. Do not place children on your lap when sliding.
  • No climbing on the outside of the play equipment.
  • No climbing up the slides.
  • No running or rough play allowed.
  • No food, gum, or drinks.
  • Please do not bring toys or other items into the play equipment.
  • Be safe and have fun!

The Woodlands Methodist Church is not responsible for any injury or accident resulting from the improper use of the playground equipment.

Please notify church staff of any problems or concerns at 832-702-0769.

Covenant Church at Creekside Indoor Playground

After Sunday worship, families are invited to enjoy our indoor playground as a place to connect, play and build community. The playground is available for children and families attending worship services and is not open to the public or available for weekday drop-in use at this time.
